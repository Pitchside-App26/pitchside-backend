How the numbers work

For each team: Over 1.5 % = league games with 2+ goals ÷ games played. Goal-in-both-halves % = games with at least one goal in each half ÷ games played (second-half goals = full-time total minus half-time total). Combined % = average of the two teams. Venue-split = the home team's home games and the away team's away games. Teams with fewer than 6 games are flagged ⚑.
