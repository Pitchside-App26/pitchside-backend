How the numbers work

For each team: Over 1.5 % = league games with 2+ goals ÷ games played. Goal-in-both-halves % = games with at least one goal in each half ÷ games played (second-half goals = full-time total minus half-time total). Each team's record is then pulled towards its league's average by 15 games' worth for Over 1.5 and 25 for GIBH: (hits + N × league rate) ÷ (games + N). Short records run hot by chance, so a 9-from-9 team in a 75% league shows 84%, not 100%; the pull fades as games build up. Combined % = average of the two teams' estimates. Venue-split = the same from the home team's home games and the away team's away games. Teams with fewer than 6 games are flagged ⚑.
